# Proposed Warranty Extension to 2 Years

FOR INTERNAL REVIEW. Not approved.

Proposed effective date: September 1, 2026.

## Proposal

Extend the TechNova limited warranty on all hardware from 1 year to
2 years from delivery, for orders placed on or after the effective date.

## Coverage

Same coverage as the current Limited Warranty. Defects in materials or
workmanship under normal use. Accidental damage and normal wear stay
excluded.

## Claims

Customers would file claims the same way, from Orders, using Warranty
claim. Claims in the second year would require proof of purchase.

## Open questions

- Expected increase in claim volume and cost
- Whether existing customers get the extension
- Supplier terms for batteries in the second year

Owner: Product Operations. Review with Finance and Legal before approval.
