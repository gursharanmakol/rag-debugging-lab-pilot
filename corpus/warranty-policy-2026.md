# Limited Warranty

TechNova hardware comes with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of
delivery.

## What's covered

Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. For example, a
headset that stops charging, a speaker with a failed driver, or a button
that no longer responds.

## What's not covered

- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracks, or liquid damage
- Normal wear, such as worn ear cushions or scratches
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Lost or stolen items

## How to make a claim

1. Open Orders and select the item.
2. Choose Warranty claim and describe the problem.
3. We may ask for a photo or video, or suggest troubleshooting steps first.

If we confirm a defect, we'll repair or replace the item at no cost. A
replacement item is covered for the rest of the original warranty period,
or 90 days, whichever is longer.

You'll need your order number or proof of purchase for any claim.
