# Shipping Policy

## Delivery options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | $5.99, free on orders over $50 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.99 |

Delivery times start when your order ships, not when you place it. Orders
placed before 2 p.m. Central Time on a business day usually ship the same
day.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. We can't ship to
P.O. boxes with Express delivery.

## Order processing

You'll get an email with a tracking number when your order ships. Large
orders may arrive in more than one package.

## Duties and taxes

Prices for Canadian orders include estimated duties and taxes, so there's
nothing extra to pay on delivery.
