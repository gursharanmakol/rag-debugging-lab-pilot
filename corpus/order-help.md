# Tracking and Managing Orders

## Where can I see my orders?

Sign in and open Orders. You'll see every order from the last two years.

## What do the order statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | We've received your order and are preparing it. |
| Packed | Your items are packed and waiting for pickup. |
| Shipped | The carrier has your package. A tracking link is available. |
| Delivered | The carrier has marked the package delivered. |

## Can I change my shipping address?

Yes, while the order is still Processing. Open the order and choose Edit
address. Once it's Packed or Shipped, the address can't be changed.

## Can I add items to an order?

No. Place a new order for the extra items.
