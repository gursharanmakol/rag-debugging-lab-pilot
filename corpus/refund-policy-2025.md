# Refund Policy

You may request a refund within 14 days of receiving your order.

To start, contact customer support with your order number. A support
agent will confirm whether the item qualifies and send you instructions.

## Refund options

- Refund to your original payment method, or
- TechNova store credit, issued immediately after the item is received.

Refunds to a card can take up to 14 business days.

Gift cards, software, and clearance items are not refundable.
