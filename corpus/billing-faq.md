# Billing Questions

## When will I be charged?

We authorize your payment when you place an order and charge it when the
order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, you may see
separate charges.

## Why do I see two charges?

One is usually a temporary authorization hold. It disappears on its own,
typically within a few business days.

## What will the charge say on my statement?

Look for TECHNOVA STORE followed by part of your order number.

## Where is my refund on my statement?

Refunds show up as a credit from TECHNOVA STORE. Timing depends on your
bank. If you don't see it after 10 business days, contact your bank first,
then contact us with your order number.

## Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Open Orders, select the order, and choose Download invoice.
