# Payment Methods

## What payment methods do you accept?

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover
- PayPal
- TechNova gift cards

## Can I split a payment?

You can combine one gift card with one card or PayPal payment. You can't
split an order across two cards.

## How do I update my saved card?

Go to Account, then Payment methods. You can add a card, remove one, or
set a default.

## My payment was declined

Check that the billing address matches the one your bank has on file. If
it still fails, your bank may have blocked the charge, so contact them
before trying again.
