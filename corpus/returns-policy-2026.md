# Returns Policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery.

## Condition of returned items

- Unopened items can be returned for a full refund.
- Opened items can be returned if they're undamaged and include all
  original parts and accessories.
- Opened electronics, including headsets, speakers, and chargers, have a
  10% restocking fee.

Items that are damaged by use, or returned without their parts, may
receive a partial refund or be sent back to you.

## How to return an item

1. Open Orders and choose Return items.
2. Print the prepaid return label. Return shipping is free.
3. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier within 14 days
   of printing the label.

## What happens next

We inspect returns within 3 business days of receiving them. Your refund
is then processed under the Refund Policy.

## Defective items

If an item stopped working because of a defect, you may be covered by our
Limited Warranty instead, with no restocking fee.
