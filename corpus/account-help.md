# Account and Sign-In Help

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter your email address.
We'll send a reset link that works for 1 hour.

## How do I change my email address?

Go to Account, then Profile, and choose Change email. We'll send a
confirmation link to the new address.

## Can I have more than one account?

Each email address can have one account. Order history can't be moved
between accounts.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Privacy, and choose Delete account. Open orders must
be delivered or cancelled first. Deletion is permanent and removes your
order history.
