# Headset Care and Troubleshooting

## Cleaning

Wipe the ear cushions and headband with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Don't
use alcohol or cleaning sprays. Replacement ear cushions are sold
separately.

## Pairing with a new device

Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue, then
select the headset in your device's Bluetooth settings. The headset
remembers up to 4 devices.

## Battery tips

- Charge fully before first use.
- Store the headset at about half charge if you won't use it for a month
  or more.
- Battery life shortens over time. This is normal.

## Still not working?

Try a reset. Hold the power and volume-down buttons together for 10
seconds. If the headset still won't charge, pair, or play sound, contact
support. It may be covered under warranty.
