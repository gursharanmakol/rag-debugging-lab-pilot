# Shipping Questions

## Where is my tracking number?

It's in your shipping confirmation email and on the order page under
Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after shipping.

## My package is late

Carriers sometimes run a day or two behind, especially around holidays.
If your package hasn't moved in 5 business days, contact us and we'll open
a trace with the carrier.

## My package says delivered but I don't have it

Check around your door, with neighbors, and in your mailbox or building's
package room. If you still can't find it after 48 hours, contact us.

## Which carriers do you use?

We use UPS and FedEx in the United States and Canada Post in Canada. The
carrier is chosen automatically based on your address.
