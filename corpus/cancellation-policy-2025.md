# Order Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of being placed.

To cancel, reply to your order confirmation email or contact customer
support. After 24 hours, orders are sent to our warehouse and can no
longer be cancelled, even if they have not shipped yet.

If you no longer want an order after this window, you can return it after
delivery.
