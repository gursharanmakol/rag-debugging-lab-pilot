# Order Cancellation Policy

You can cancel an order at any time before it ships.

## How to cancel

Open Orders, select the order, and choose Cancel order. The Cancel order
button stays available until the order ships. If you don't see it, the
order has already shipped.

When you cancel before shipping, you won't be charged. If a payment was
already authorized, the hold is released by your bank, usually within a
few business days.

## After your order ships

Once an order ships, it can't be cancelled. You can still send it back
under our Returns Policy after it arrives. If you refuse the delivery,
the carrier returns it to us and we process it as a return.

## Partial cancellations

If only some items in your order have shipped, you can cancel the items
that haven't shipped yet from the same Orders page.
