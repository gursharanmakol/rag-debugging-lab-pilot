# Refund Policy

If you're not happy with a TechNova purchase, you can request a refund
within 30 days of delivery.

## How to request a refund

1. Sign in and open Orders.
2. Select the order and choose Request a refund.
3. Tell us which items you're returning and why.

Most items need to come back to us before we issue the refund. See the
Returns Policy for how to send an item back.

## When you'll get your money

We refund to your original payment method once we receive and check the
item. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on
your bank or card issuer.

If you paid with a gift card, the amount goes back to that gift card.

## Items we can't refund

- Gift cards
- Digital downloads that have been redeemed
- Items marked as final sale

## Partial refunds

If an item comes back damaged or missing parts, we may issue a partial
refund. We'll email you with the reason before we process it.
